The Nvidia CEO scandal, day by day.

On 8 November 2026, Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang told staff he had an extramarital affair. Press reports identify the other person as Alice Huang, an executive linked to Nvidia customer Megaspeed.

U.S. intelligence assessments reportedly state that she is a Chinese intelligence asset. They allege she used her ties to Huang to circumvent US export controls and pass information to Beijing.

As it stands, the matter is being looked into by the Nvidia Board of Directors, Congress and the FBI. China has denied any involvement.
